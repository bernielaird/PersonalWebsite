How it works

  • From the round you pick, the rest of the map is played out {{ meta.maps_per_option }} times for each buy. In later rounds both teams buy the way pro teams usually do with that money.
  • Who wins each round comes from a model of pro rounds since 2023 (both teams' guns, armor and spend, the score and the round); money comes from real pro incomes and loss bonuses.
  • Bonus is the round right after a pistol win: keep the pistol-round guns and add a little. A half buy keeps enough to full buy next round even if you lose; a force doesn't.
  • Guns kept are the guns your survivors carried out of last round: about 3,300 credits' worth for “1–2 guns” and 9,800 for “3+”. They lower what a full buy costs. Odds are averaged over the current map pool, and money between the precomputed levels is interpolated.
  • Each figure is about ±2 points from simulation noise, so buys within 2 points of each other are a coin flip.
