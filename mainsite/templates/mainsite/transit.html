visual transit simulator

Buses and trains running two lines around the University of Minnesota: passengers show up at stops, vehicles pick them up and drop them off, and each vehicle reports its load and CO₂. Set how long to run and how often vehicles leave, then press Start. Click a vehicle to follow it, or pick a line to report an issue on it.

The simulation is the original Java code, compiled to JavaScript with TeaVM so it runs in your browser; it used to need a Java server and a websocket. Built for CSCI 3081W at the University of Minnesota (Fall 2024) with Kaden Lin and Kenny: the course staff provided the base simulator and its GUI, and our team extended it (vehicle colors, refactoring, tests and documentation). I worked on the visualization module.
