Hi, I'm Bernie!

I'm a software engineer who likes building things end to end: collecting messy data, turning it into something clean and trustworthy, and putting it in front of people. I finished my B.S. in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota, and I'm now working on my M.S. in Computer Science at Georgia Tech, specializing in computing systems.

What I'm working on

My main project is VCT Win Probability. It scrapes every pro Valorant match since 2023, models each team's chance of winning the round after every kill, and credits each swing to the players who caused it. It runs on this site and updates itself as new matches are played.

Before that I built MusicElo, which ranks your music through head-to-head matchups using your Spotify library, and this website.

How I work

I like code that's simple to read and easy to trust: validated data, tests where they matter, and results I can check. Most of what I know comes from building real projects and then going back to make them better, usually well after they're "done."

What I'm looking for

Software engineering roles, especially backend, data or machine learning work, where I can contribute early, learn fast and work on real problems. If that sounds like your team, get in touch.
